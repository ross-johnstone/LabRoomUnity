Hybrid room - asset credits
===========================
Built from the lab scan: positions/sizes of all furniture and props, carpet and oak textures,
posters, whiteboard, sign and screen images (taken from the photogrammetry scan itself).
Procedural (made for this project): room shell, window, desks, sofa, armchair, sideboard, shelf unit,
TV, monitors, keyboards, laptops, bottles, cans, mugs, coat stand, mushroom-mug lamp.
